A Message from the Founders

Dear Valued Customers,

Today marks an exciting new chapter in our journey.

First, we want to thank you. Your trust, loyalty, and feedback have helped shape our software and our company. Every enhancement we’ve made over the years has been driven by one goal—to help freight brokers work more efficiently, serve customers better, and grow their businesses.

As we push forward to deliver on that goal, we’re excited to announce that EZLoader TMS is becoming EZ TMS.

While our name is changing, our mission is not. We’re still committed to delivering a reliable TMS built specifically for freight brokers—one that is powerful, easy to use, and focused on helping your team accomplish more with less effort.

Our new brand reflects where we’re headed

The freight industry is changing rapidly, and we’re making significant investments to ensure EZ TMS remains at the forefront of innovation. Artificial Intelligence, automation, and business intelligence are becoming fundamental tools for modern brokerages, and we’re building them directly into the platform you already know and trust.

Over the coming months, you’ll see exciting enhancements across EZ TMS, including:

  • Intelligent order ingestion and load building that automates repetitive data entry and streamlines shipment creation.
  • AI-powered quoting that helps brokers make faster, more informed pricing decisions.
  • Smarter automation throughout the platform to eliminate unnecessary clicks and simplify everyday workflows.

On the roadmap right behind these enhancements, EZ TMS will be focused on the following:

  • Proactive exception management that alerts your team before issues become customer problems.
  • Enhanced reporting and analytics with dynamic dashboards and expanded KPI visibility, giving every department the insights needed to improve performance, profitability, and customer service.

Our vision is simple: technology should empower brokers—not create more work. That’s why every investment we make is focused on helping your team save time, make better decisions, and scale your business with confidence.

We’re building more than a Transportation Management System. We’re building an intelligent operating platform tailored for freight brokers!

Thank you for believing in us and growing with us. We are incredibly grateful for your partnership and excited for what’s ahead.

The name is changing. Our commitment to your success never will.

Welcome to EZ TMS.
Less Clicks. More Growth.

Best Regards,
Brendon Wilson — Co-Founder & Owner
Steven Smith — Co-Founder & Owner
