3 Signs You’ve Outgrown Your TMS

Most brokerages don’t outgrow their TMS in a single dramatic moment. It happens quietly — an extra tab here, a workaround spreadsheet there — until one day the software you bought to move faster is the thing slowing you down. If any of the following sounds familiar, it may be time to take a hard look.

1. Closing a load takes too many tools

Count the number of screens it takes to move one load from quote to invoice. If your team is jumping between a rating tool, a tracking site, a document folder, an accounting package, and a spreadsheet to tie it all together, every one of those handoffs is a place where time leaks and errors creep in.

A modern TMS should carry a load through its entire lifecycle in one place:

  • Quote and rate against live market data
  • Tender to qualified carriers
  • Track, document, and update the customer
  • Invoice — automatically, on the customer’s schedule

2. Your pricing punishes growth

Per-load fees and per-feature gates feel small at first. Then volume climbs, and suddenly the tool that helped you grow is taking a cut of every win. Flat, per-seat pricing keeps your software cost predictable and your incentives aligned — you should never have to think twice about running one more load through the system.

If your TMS is slowing your brokerage down, it’s costing you margin.

3. Support treats you like a ticket number

When something breaks at 4:55 on a Friday, you need a person who knows freight — not a queue. The right partner sets up new agents quickly, answers the phone, and actually understands the workflow you’re describing. If your current support experience is a black hole, that’s a sign you’ve outgrown more than the software.

None of these problems mean you did anything wrong. They’re the natural result of a growing brokerage pushing against the limits of a tool built for where you were, not where you’re headed.
